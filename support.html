Support

We’re here if something’s wrong.

Minutewell is on the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac. There is no account to reset, because there is no account.

Email minutewellsupport@hokfam.com and include the device and what you expected to happen.

A few things the app already does

  • Delete a recording from its screen. The audio and notes go with it.
  • Turn off “Your API key” in Settings if you don’t want transcript text sent to a provider you configured.
  • Restore purchases from Settings or the Pro screen. Purchases are handled by Apple.
